ABOUT

Spice Haven was born in 2010 from a simple belief — that great Indian food should be accessible, authentic, and deeply nourishing. Our kitchen draws on recipes passed down through three generations, refined with the finest fresh produce and spices sourced directly from India.

Every dish on our menu tells a story — of regions, rituals, and the remarkable diversity of Indian culinary culture. From the slow-braised kormas of the north to coastal seafood curries, we bring the full spectrum of flavour to your table.

We are proud to have served over 50,000 guests in the heart of San Francisco. Our doors are always open — come discover your new favourite dish.
